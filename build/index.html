Behind the work

How I build.

I connect design, code and AI to turn an idea into a product people can use. Behind the work is a system I built to collaborate with AI agents. Here’s how I use it — and the decisions I make myself.

I connect
the pieces.

I’m interested in how people think, what they need and what makes a solution easy to use. I bring that perspective into both design and development.

I work across the whole product: from the need and user experience to the data and functionality that make it work. When something falls short in practice, I can make changes across those disciplines.

The next task shouldn’t
start from scratch.

I’ve built my own AI harness — a system that gives agents project context, clear tasks and procedures to follow. It preserves decisions and working methods so I can build on what previous work has taught me.

I set the direction, assess the results and decide what moves forward. What I want to bring to a team is a way of working that makes decisions understandable and work easier to pick up and develop further.

I build, use
and improve.

I built NYME for my own job search. It helps me research roles and prepare targeted applications. It has helped me get job interviews, and I use that experience to improve the workflow.

Other projects serve other people’s needs: an external organisation uses Argus daily for document work, and others have started using Sitter. That gives me experience of what it takes for a solution to work beyond my own screen.
