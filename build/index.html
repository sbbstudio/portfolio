01 / How I think

Explore → Decompose → Understand
Build → Evaluate → Iterate

Understanding comes
before implementation.

I start with the problem, the person using the system and the outcome that matters. Then I break the problem into parts I can investigate.

I make assumptions explicit, test them and change direction when the evidence asks me to.

02 / Operating range

01 → Understand

Product & business

Who needs this? What should change? I connect the user’s problem to a useful outcome and a scope we can actually build.

02 → Shape

Design & experience

Turn that understanding into a clear flow, a usable interface and a visual language that belongs to the work.

03 → Build

Code, data & AI

Connect interfaces, APIs, data and automation. Understand the boundaries, evaluate the output and check the complete flow.

04 → Learn ↺

Systems & growth

Put the work in front of people. Look at what happens, find the friction and bring that learning into the next iteration.

03 / AI changed the equation

Ask → Generate → Inspect → Understand
Test → Challenge → Refine

More reach.
The same responsibility.

AI helps me research, challenge ideas, implement, debug and test. It lets me take a wider understanding further into a working product.

I still need to inspect the output, understand the important parts and decide what is ready to ship.

04 / How I build

Problem → Research → Architecture → Modules
Implementation → Evaluation → System log → Iteration

The system
comes with me.

I don’t start from zero. Every project leaves behind a better way to work — clearer decisions, reusable components and stronger checks. The next one starts from there.

I break the work into modules, record the important decisions and get a complete flow working early. Then I test the weak points and iterate.

AI’s role. Research and tailored application material are part of the product itself.
