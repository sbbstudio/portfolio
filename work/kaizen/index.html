Design process · 1 of 7

KAIZENHow I designed it.

Product design, brand and build. Solo, 2025–26.

Problem
I need to make an unfamiliar service concrete enough for a small business to consider using it.
Decision in this section
A character can attract attention while leaving the service unexplained. I pair the introduction with concrete jobs: preparing quotes, drafting replies and following up customers.

The offer · 2 of 7

02

Put the agreement in view.

Starting point
I’m asking a business to consider handing over part of its work.
What I saw
The price is only part of that decision. Scope, existing systems and approval also need explaining.
Decision
I keep the scope, installation price and payment condition together in the offer.

The first task · 3 of 7

03

Start with work they recognise.

Starting point
I’m asking the visitor to describe a task worth handing over.
What I saw
That first conversation should be possible without AI knowledge or a prepared brief.
Decision
I use three cards to explain where to start, how suitability is assessed and what preparation is needed.

The method · 4 of 7

04

Show how the work is agreed and checked.

Starting point
I’m explaining how a task becomes a working service.
What I saw
The visitor needs to see what they approve and how the finished work is assessed.
Decision
I present four steps, from agreeing the scope to checking the work together.

The invitation · 5 of 7

05

Make one task enough.

Starting point
I’m turning the explanation into an invitation to talk.
What I saw
The first conversation can begin with a single task. The invitation needs no larger commitment.
Decision
I give it a dedicated section and state that the conversation carries no obligation.

Contact · 6 of 7

06

Leave room for a question.

Starting point
I’m giving visitors a way to ask for help.
What I saw
Someone may have a question before they can name a task.
Decision
I offer phone, email and a short form, alongside a clear invitation to ask.

Result · 7 of 7

07

The page connects the offer to direct contact options.

Result
Next, I would test whether visitors can explain the service and identify a task they would bring to the first conversation.
